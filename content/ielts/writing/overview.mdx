## Task 1 structure to learn

The source sheet teaches one four-part structure and applies it to every
Task 1 chart type. It is reproduced here rather than on the individual chart
pages because the source never varies it by chart.

### 1. Introduction

**Purpose** — rephrase the task using synonyms and slightly change the
sentence structure.

**Key vocab & phrases**

- illustrates, presents, shows, compares, depicts
- the number of, the amount of, the proportion of
- between… and…, during the period of…

**Sample sentence** — "The chart **illustrates** the percentage of adults who
exercised regularly between 2000 and 2020."

### 2. Overview

**Purpose** — highlight the main trend or key feature without mentioning
specific numbers.

**Key vocab & phrases**

- Overall, it is clear that…
- The most noticeable trend is…
- A general upward/downward trend is observed
- The majority/minority of…

**Sample sentence** — "**Overall**, it is clear that online sales increased
dramatically, while physical store sales steadily declined."

### 3. Body paragraph 1

**Purpose** — describe main features, categories, or changes over time.

**Trend verbs & adverbs**

- increased, rose, climbed, grew
- sharply, steadily, significantly, slightly
- peaked at, reached a high of

**Supporting phrases** — starting at…, followed by…, then, subsequently, in
the following year.

**Sample sentence** — "The number of users **rose sharply** from 20% in 2015
to a peak of 55% in 2019."

### 4. Body paragraph 2

**Purpose** — add details about other categories or make comparisons.

**Comparison & stability vocab**

- in contrast, on the other hand, whereas, similarly
- remained stable, fluctuated, maintained the same level
- accounted for, represented

**Sample sentence** — "**In contrast,** the rural population remained stable
at around 30%, while the urban percentage fluctuated slightly."

## Grammar

**Tense** — use past tense if the graph shows past years (e.g. *increased,
decreased, remained*).

**Common mistakes**

- ❌ Giving personal opinions
- ❌ Skipping the overview paragraph
- ❌ Listing data without analysis

## Chart types

Visual forms named by the source:

- Line Graph
- Bar Chart
- Pie Chart
- Table
- Process Diagram
- Map Comparison
