## Part 3: Two-way discussion (4–5 min)

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Focus | In-depth discussion on abstract, related topics |
| Example questions | Why do people enjoy fiction? How can reading benefit children? |
| Tip | Give thoughtful answers, explain with examples or reasons |

From the preparation strategy: **give deeper, thoughtful answers — add
examples.** Part 3 questions connect back to the Part 2 topic.

## Question bank

*From the [Speaking Sample Questions](https://app.notion.com/p/cf0f75584dea822b8a5b01b7e5f0724d)
sub-page, split by part.*

<details>
<summary>Hometown</summary>

- What are the advantages of living in a small town compared to a big city?
- Why do some people prefer to move to cities from rural areas?
- Do you think governments should invest more in developing small towns?
- How does urbanization affect the environment of a town?
- In your opinion, what makes a city or town a good place to live?

</details>

<details>
<summary>Education</summary>

- What makes a good teacher in your opinion?
- How has education changed in your country in recent years?
- Do you think students today face more pressure than before?
- What are the advantages and disadvantages of online learning?
- Should education be free for everyone? Why or why not?
- How important is critical thinking in modern education?

</details>

<details>
<summary>Work-Life Balance</summary>

- Why do people nowadays struggle more with work-life balance than before?
- What role do companies or employers play in helping workers maintain a healthy balance?
- Do you think remote working helps people achieve better balance?
- How does poor work-life balance affect mental health?
- What strategies can individuals use to manage their time more effectively?
- Should governments encourage companies to offer more flexible working hours?

</details>

<details>
<summary>Culture</summary>

- Why is it important to preserve cultural traditions?
- How has globalization affected local cultures?
- Do young people in your country value their culture as much as older generations?
- Should schools teach more about local culture and traditions?
- Can cultural differences cause misunderstandings? How?
- Is it possible to respect other cultures while maintaining your own identity?

</details>

<details>
<summary>Environment</summary>

- What are the main environmental problems in your country?
- What can individuals do to help improve the environment?
- Do you think governments are doing enough to tackle climate change?
- How can schools help students become more environmentally aware?
- Is economic development always in conflict with environmental protection?
- Should companies be punished for polluting the environment?

</details>

<details>
<summary>Health</summary>

- What are the most common health problems in your country?
- How can people be encouraged to live a healthier lifestyle?
- Should governments spend more on healthcare or education?
- How has modern technology affected our health, both positively and negatively?
- Do you think mental health is taken seriously in your country?
- Who should be responsible for public health: individuals or the government?

</details>

<details>
<summary>News</summary>

- How has the way people consume news changed in recent years?
- Do you think the media can influence public opinion? How?
- Is it more important to focus on local news or international news?
- Should governments have control over media content? Why or why not?
- How can people identify fake news or misinformation?
- Do you think social media is a reliable source of news?

</details>

<details>
<summary>Globalization</summary>

- How does globalization affect local culture?
- Do you think globalization creates more opportunities or more problems?
- What are the advantages of global trade?
- Can globalization widen the gap between rich and poor countries?
- Should countries try to protect their own industries from globalization?

</details>

<details>
<summary>Crime & Law</summary>

- What are the main causes of crime in modern society?
- Do you think harsher punishments reduce crime?
- How can governments prevent young people from committing crimes?
- What is the difference between minor and serious crimes?
- Should all laws be the same in every country?

</details>

<details>
<summary>Success & Goals</summary>

- Why do some people succeed while others don't?
- Is success more about hard work or luck?
- Do schools teach children how to set goals effectively?
- What kind of goals do most people have in your country?
- How has the definition of success changed over time?

</details>
