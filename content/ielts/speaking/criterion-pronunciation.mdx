## What the source says to do

- **Stress key words, vary intonation.**
- Be clear, natural, and varied in your intonation.
- **Be expressive** — add emotion and intonation, sound interested in what
  you're saying.
- **Shadow native speakers** — copy their pace, stress, rhythm.
