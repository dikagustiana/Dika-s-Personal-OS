## What the source says to do

- Use topic-related words and paraphrasing.
- Use a wide range of vocabulary, including idiomatic language.
- **Aim to use 3–4 advanced vocab items naturally in every answer.**

Build topic sheets of useful phrases:

- **Education** — "lifelong learning", "academic pressure"
- **Environment** — "carbon footprint", "sustainable practices"
- **Hometown** — "close-knit community", "urban sprawl"
- **Work** — "work-life balance", "career advancement"

## What to check for in feedback

- Repetition of basic vocab ("good", "bad", "nice").
