## Part 1: Introduction & interview (4–5 min)

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Focus | Personal information, daily routines, likes/dislikes |
| Common topics | Hobbies, hometown, studies, work, family |
| Example questions | What do you usually do on weekends? Do you enjoy your job? |
| Tip | Keep answers short but natural (2–3 sentences), avoid overthinking. |

From the preparation strategy: **Part 1 wants short, fluent personal answers.**
Practise by recording yourself daily answering three Part 1 questions.

## Question bank

*From the [Speaking Sample Questions](https://app.notion.com/p/cf0f75584dea822b8a5b01b7e5f0724d)
sub-page, split by part. The Part 2 cue cards and Part 3 questions from the

<details>
<summary>Hometown</summary>

- Where is your hometown?
- What do you like most about your hometown?
- Has your hometown changed much over the years?
- Is it a good place for young people to live?
- What kind of jobs do people do in your hometown?
- Do you think you will live there in the future?

</details>

<details>
<summary>Education</summary>

- What subject did you enjoy most at school?
- What kind of school did you go to?
- Do you think education is important?
- How many years have you spent in education so far?
- Do you prefer studying alone or with others?
- Are there any subjects you would like to learn more about?

</details>

<details>
<summary>Work-Life Balance</summary>

- Do you have a busy daily schedule?
- How do you usually spend your evenings after work or study?
- Do you think it's important to have a balance between work and relaxation?
- How do you usually relax after a stressful day?
- Have you ever felt overwhelmed by work or study?

</details>

<details>
<summary>Culture</summary>

- What culture do you belong to?
- Are cultural traditions important in your family?
- Do you enjoy learning about other cultures?
- Have you ever experienced culture shock?
- What's your favorite thing about your culture?

</details>

<details>
<summary>Environment</summary>

- Do you care about the environment?
- What do you do to help protect the environment?
- Are there any environmental problems in your area?
- Do you recycle things in your home?
- Have your habits changed in recent years because of environmental issues?

</details>

<details>
<summary>Health</summary>

- How often do you exercise?
- What do you do to stay healthy?
- Do you think you have a healthy lifestyle?
- Have your eating habits changed recently?
- Is it easy to stay healthy in your country?

</details>

<details>
<summary>News</summary>

- How often do you read or watch the news?
- What kind of news are you most interested in?
- Do you prefer reading the news online or in print? Why?
- Have your news habits changed in recent years?
- Is it important to stay updated with the news?

</details>

<details>
<summary>Globalization</summary>

- Have you ever heard the term globalization before?
- In what ways is your country influenced by globalization?
- Do you think globalization is good or bad? Why?
- Do you use products from other countries regularly?
- Do you enjoy foreign food, movies, or fashion?

</details>

<details>
<summary>Crime & Law</summary>

- Is crime a big problem in your country?
- Have you ever been affected by crime?
- Do you think people feel safe in your city?
- What do you think makes people commit crimes?
- Are there strict laws in your country?

</details>

<details>
<summary>Success & Goals</summary>

- What does success mean to you?
- Do you think people define success differently?
- Do you have personal goals you're working toward?
- How do you stay motivated to reach your goals?
- Do you think setting goals is important?

</details>
