**Smooth flow of ideas, logical structure, minimal hesitation.**

## What the source says to do

- **Don't aim for perfect grammar. Keep talking confidently.**
- Speak smoothly and logically.
- Avoid one-word answers, memorized templates, and long pauses.

## What to check for in feedback

- Filler words ("umm", "like") overuse.
