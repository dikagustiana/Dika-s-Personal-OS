## Part 2: Long turn (3–4 min incl. 1 min prep)

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Focus | Speak for 1–2 minutes based on a prompt |
| Task | You'll be given a cue card and 1 minute to prepare |
| Example prompt | Describe a book that changed your perspective… |
| Tip | Structure: Past > Description > Feelings > Why it matters |

From the preparation strategy: **timed 1-minute prep, 2-minute talk.** Use
IELTS cue cards to practise storytelling.

## Cue cards

*From the [Speaking Sample Questions](https://app.notion.com/p/cf0f75584dea822b8a5b01b7e5f0724d)
sub-page. You will be given a card and asked to speak for up to 2 minutes.*

<details>
<summary>Hometown</summary>

**Describe your hometown.** You should say:

- where it is
- what it is known for
- what people do there
- and explain how you feel about your hometown.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Education</summary>

**Describe a teacher who has greatly influenced your life.** You should say:

- who the teacher is
- what subject they taught
- what you remember about them
- and explain how they influenced you.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Work-Life Balance</summary>

**Describe a time when you struggled to balance work and life.** You should say:

- when it happened
- what you had to do
- how it affected your personal life
- and explain how you managed the situation.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Culture</summary>

**Describe a cultural tradition in your country that you find interesting.**
You should say:

- what the tradition is
- when and where it is celebrated
- what people do during the tradition
- and explain why you find it interesting.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Environment</summary>

**Describe a place you know that has been affected by pollution.** You should say:

- where this place is
- what kind of pollution is found there
- what effects the pollution has had
- and explain how you feel about this issue.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Health</summary>

**Describe a time when you were ill or injured.** You should say:

- when it happened
- what your symptoms were
- how you recovered
- and explain how you felt during that time.

</details>

<details>
<summary>News</summary>

**Describe a piece of news you recently read or heard about.** You should say:

- what the news was about
- where you saw or heard it
- how it affected you
- and explain why you remember this particular news.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Globalization</summary>

**Describe a way in which globalization has affected your life.** You should say:

- what it is
- how it has changed your daily life
- whether the change is positive or negative
- and explain how you feel about this influence.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Crime & Law</summary>

**Describe a law in your country that you agree with.** You should say:

- what the law is
- how you know about it
- who it affects
- and explain why you think this law is a good one.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Success & Goals</summary>

**Describe a goal you set for yourself and achieved.** You should say:

- what the goal was
- how you achieved it
- how long it took
- and explain how you felt after achieving it.

</details>
